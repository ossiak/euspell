Welcome to Euspell

Euspell rewrites English web pages (and PDFs) into reformed spelling — entirely on your device. No account, no telemetry, and the text you read is never sent anywhere. (Dictation is the exception: it uses your browser's own speech recognition, which transcribes in the cloud.)

To read and convert the pages you visit, Euspell needs permission to access websites.

You’re all set — open any English page and it’ll appear in euspell.

Pin Euspell to your toolbar.

The icon is also the on/off readout: the blue mark while Euspell is converting, inverted while it’s off.

You can change site access any time from the extension’s Options page.
